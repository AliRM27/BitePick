Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026. Learn how we handle and protect your personal information.

At BitePick, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information we process. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how it is used, and the steps we take to protect your data when you use the BitePick mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

BitePick is designed to serve you recommendations with minimal data friction. We process the following types of information:

  • Precise Geolocation: To suggest nearby cafes and restaurants, we require access to your device's GPS coordinates. This is only accessed with your explicit consent.
  • Device & Usage Data: We track anonymous event data such as when location permissions are granted or denied, which categories (food/coffee) are selected, and general performance logs.
  • Preference Parameters: Preferences such as your chosen distance radius (e.g., 1 km, 3 km) and preferred map routing provider (Apple Maps or Google Maps).

2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information strictly to enable the app's functionality and monitor its performance:

  • To retrieve nearby points of interest matching your selected context.
  • To display relative distance and compute basic walk or drive travel estimates.
  • To analyze overall app performance and error counts, allowing us to debug API bottlenecks.
  • We never sell or rent your precise coordinates or personal details to third-party advertisers.

3. Third-Party Services

BitePick integrates with external routing and map providers to offer navigation links. When you tap "Take me there," the app initiates a query to:

  • Apple Maps: For iOS devices (subject to Apple's privacy guidelines).
  • Google Maps: For Android and web integrations (subject to Google's privacy policy).
  • Analytics Providers: We use lightweight analytics tools to record application crashes and usage volumes.

4. Data Retention and Security

Your coordinates are processed on-the-fly to fetch restaurant options and are not stored in our database profiles permanently. General analytics events are recorded anonymously and kept for system enhancement purposes. We enforce industry-standard security protocols to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or distribution of service metadata.

5. Your Rights and Preferences

You maintain full authority over your data. You can enable or disable location tracking at any point via your mobile device system settings. Deactivating location tracking will prevent BitePick from gathering real-time data, though you may still manually simulate coordinates if the option is supported.

6. Policy Updates

We may modify this Privacy Policy as our features evolve. We will post modifications directly on this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this policy, please reach out to us at lotustudio.app@gmail.com or via our Contact Form.
